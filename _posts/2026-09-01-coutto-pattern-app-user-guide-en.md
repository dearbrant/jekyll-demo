**Calibration + Projection: Start Projector Sewing in Two Steps**

## Preparation: Mirror Your Phone to the Projector

**Connection Methods**

- **Wired Connection**: Connect with an HDMI cable. The connection is stable and reliable, and the signal transmission quality is high, making it suitable for long working sessions.
- **Wireless Connection**: Some projectors support wireless casting, such as Screen Mirroring AirPlay on iOS and Chromecast on Android. Wireless casting is flexible and convenient.

![Wired HDMI connection]({{ "/images/blog/coutto-pattern-app-user-guide/img-012.png" | relative_url }})

![Wireless casting from a phone]({{ "/images/blog/coutto-pattern-app-user-guide/img-014.png" | relative_url }})

## Step 1: Calibration

The Button Pattern App provides a quick way to calibrate the projected image.

### Quick Calibration in Two Steps

1. Drag the four corners of the grid in the app so that they align with the grid on the cutting mat. Continue until the projected grid and the cutting-mat grid are fully aligned.
2. Read the height and width covered by the projected grid on the cutting mat, then enter the measured length and width in the Button Pattern App.

![Aligning the projected grid with the cutting mat]({{ "/images/blog/coutto-pattern-app-user-guide/img-021.png" | relative_url }})

![Calibration interface in the app]({{ "/images/blog/coutto-pattern-app-user-guide/img-020.png" | relative_url }})

## Step 2: Projection

The Button Pattern App can project patterns onto fabric at the correct scale.

- **Color Inversion**: Invert the pattern colors to improve the visibility of pattern lines on different fabrics.
- **Pattern Operations**: Easily rotate, flip and mirror patterns to suit different cutting needs.

![Pattern projected onto fabric]({{ "/images/blog/coutto-pattern-app-user-guide/img-027.png" | relative_url }})

![Pattern operations in the app]({{ "/images/blog/coutto-pattern-app-user-guide/img-026.png" | relative_url }})

## Optional: Tiling

If your PDF pattern is A4 size and normally needs to be printed and assembled before use, you can use the Button Pattern App's digital tiling feature to join pattern pieces divided across several pages into complete pieces.

### Complete PDF Tiling in Three Steps

1. Adjust the number of columns used to assemble the pattern pieces.
2. If necessary, insert blank pages to align the assembled pattern pieces.
3. Adjust the vertical and horizontal overlap between pieces so that they align.

## Finally: Cutting

Cut the fabric along the pattern lines projected onto it.

### Cutting Recommendations

- **Check Again**: Before cutting, confirm again that the projection scale is correct. Use a ruler to measure the sample scale in the pattern calibration area. The measured value should equal the marked value.
- **Dim the Lights**: Make the projected pattern clearer.
- **Use a Sharp Cutting Tool**: Cut with a rotary cutter or compact electric scissors.
- **Hold the Fabric Firmly**: Use pattern weights to hold down the fabric and ensure that it does not move.

## Summary: Projector Sewing Makes Sewing Easier

Projector sewing projects digital patterns directly onto fabric for fast cutting. Setup and digital calibration can be completed in just a few minutes, greatly simplifying the traditional process of using paper patterns.

- **Save Time**: No need to print, assemble or store paper patterns.
- **Improve Accuracy**: Digital calibration ensures accurate pattern dimensions.
- **Adjust Flexibly**: Easily rotate, flip and resize patterns.
- **Eco-Friendly and Economical**: Reduce paper waste and lower material costs.
- **Save Space**: No longer necessary to store large quantities of paper patterns.
